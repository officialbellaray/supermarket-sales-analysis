# Supermarket Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes historical supermarket sales data from three branches in Yangon, Mandalay, and Naypyitaw, covering January 1 to March 30, 2019.

My goal was to understand sales performance, compare product lines and branches, and communicate business insights through a dashboard.

## Objectives

* Analyze key sales performance indicators.
* Compare sales across product lines.
* Evaluate branch performance.
* Examine sales trends over time.
* Explore customer-type and payment-method distributions.
* Present findings through a web-based dashboard.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel — data inspection, cleaning checks, PivotTables, and KPI summaries.
* AI-assisted dashboard development — dashboard design and implementation.
* GitHub — planned project storage and portfolio presentation.

## Key Findings

* Food and beverages recorded the highest product-line sales at approximately 56,145.
* Naypyitaw recorded the highest branch sales at 110,568.71.
* Members accounted for 50.8% of sales.
* Cash accounted for 34.7% of sales by payment method.
* March covers only the period through March 30, so it is not a complete calendar month.

## Data Quality and Limitations

The dashboard reports 1,000 unique invoices, zero missing source values, and exact matches between the raw and cleaned common fields.

The reported gross income equals the tax field. I therefore do not treat it as verified operating or net profit. The original external dataset source and currency also remain unconfirmed.

## Business Recommendations

* Investigate the factors contributing to strong food and beverages sales.
* Review health and beauty sales to identify possible improvement opportunities.
* Examine the practices and product mix associated with Naypyitaw's performance.
* Investigate branch rating differences.
* Verify the financial definitions before making profitability decisions.
